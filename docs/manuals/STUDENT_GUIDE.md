# Student guide

[Project overview](../../README.md) · [Reviewer tour](../DEMO.md) · [Documentation hub](../README.md)

EMPIRE OS helps adults turn a 90-day goal into small daily missions. The current version is a local demonstration; real accounts, hosting and payment-provider verification are still pending. For a safe sample walkthrough, start with the [demo guide](../DEMO.md).

## Start a season

1. Open `/` and choose **Find your archetype**.
2. Select your age bracket. The under-18 path ends without saving quiz answers.
3. Answer the 11 questions, including your goal, pillar order, daily minutes, budget and equipment. Single-choice answers move forward; use the back control to revise earlier answers.
4. Review your archetype and the plan explanation. Choose **Start Season 1**.
5. Complete the press-and-hold oath. With reduced motion enabled, use **Take the oath** instead.
6. Save your plan on the sign-up screen. Live use is designed for an email one-time code or Google; **Test sign-in (local test mode)** belongs only to a local demonstration.

The quiz preview does not require an account. Once a season is saved, **HQ** at `/hq` is your daily starting point.

## Finish, change or skip today's work

Each mission ticket shows its pillar, title, minutes and XP. Open the ticket to read the instructions before doing the task, then choose **Mark done**. Completing work stamps the ticket and updates your activity totals.

| Situation | Action in the mission details |
| --- | --- |
| You marked a mission done by mistake | Open the completed ticket and choose **Undo, mark not done** |
| You want a different mission | Choose **Swap for another mission**; one swap is available per day |
| You cannot do it today | Choose **Skip**, then **No time**, **Not relevant** or **Too hard** |
| You opened Skip by mistake | Choose **Keep it** |

Completed missions count towards XP, rank and activity. At least one finished mission makes an active day for your streak. A missed day can pause the streak while retaining XP and rank. Eligible Season Pass access supplies a weekly streak shield automatically. XP measures activity inside the app, not your value or a guaranteed real-world result.

## Use the four tabs

| Tab | What to do there |
| --- | --- |
| **HQ** (`/hq`) | See this week's theme, today's missions, score, streak, rank, new medals and weekly-review reminders |
| **Path** (`/path`) | View **Your campaign**, its three acts and weekly themes; inspect mission packs |
| **Stats** (`/stats`) | Read missions done, XP, current/best streak, your twelve medals, XP by day and completion by pillar |
| **Me** (`/me`) | View access status, journal, invitations, share cards, plan settings, college membership and data controls |

Minutes in Stats come from the recorded durations of completed missions; they are not a background timer. Charts offer a table view for reading their values. Medals are worked out from the same records each time, so they always match your history; Stats shows the date each one was earned (War Room and Boss beaten show "Earned" without a date).

## Finish a season and start the next

After Day 90, HQ shows **Season 1 complete**: days active out of 90, missions done, time invested, your best streak in the season and your medals, all from your own records. Choose **Share your season** for the image card, or **Start Season 2**. The new season starts that day from Day 1 with your current plan. Your XP, rank and medals come with you; Stats and Path switch to the new season. There is no second free week: you continue on Free (one mission a day) unless you have Season Pass.

## Review your week

The **War Room** opens from day 7 and then weekly. Use the prompt in HQ or **Me → War Room: review your week**.

Answer **This week felt**, **Biggest blocker**, **Anything changed?** and **Next week's focus**. If you select a changed schedule, budget, city or goal, supply its new value. Choose **Set next week**, then read the result explanation, headed **Reset, don't quit.** after a low week or **What changed and why** otherwise.

A city or goal change can show **Apply new plan?**. Choose **Apply new plan** or **Keep my current plan**. Your history, XP and rank stay. On Free, the result previews the adjustments and offers **Unlock with Season Pass**.

## Edit your plan

Under **Me → Your plan**, choose **Edit my plan**. You can change the main goal, pillar order, daily time, budget, city, gym access and laptop access. Select all four pillars in priority order, then choose **Save changes for tomorrow**.

The screen shows the pending start date. These edits affect future planning rather than rewriting past days.

## Understand access and reservations

The first seven days have full access. When that access ends, Free keeps one mission a day in a pillar you choose. Later Path weeks and packs remain locked; XP and ranks continue.

**Me → See Season Pass** opens the offer. In the current default reserve mode, choose **Reserve founder price** or **Reserve Season Pass** only to save the displayed price. The confirmation explicitly says **No payment taken**. A reservation does not unlock paid access. **Maybe later** returns you to the app.

Actual payment requires the separately configured and verified checkout. Never treat a local stand-in payment as a real purchase.

## Join or leave a college batch

1. In **Me → College season**, enter the **Batch code** supplied by your training and placement cell and choose **Continue**. A college join link can also lead you to this flow.
2. Read exactly what will be shared. Pick **Your section** if requested; optionally add a last-name initial so the college can distinguish similar names.
3. Choose **Join and share my progress**, or **Maybe later** to leave without joining.

The college sees your mission progress and chosen display name; your journal and quiz answers are excluded. You can belong to one batch at a time. To leave, choose **Leave this batch**, read the consequence and confirm **Leave the batch**. Your account, XP and history stay; the college stops seeing your progress. Any Season Pass access supplied by that batch ends.

## Journal, invite and share

**Me → One line for today** accepts up to 280 characters. Choose **Save**. This is a private journal and is not included in college reporting or share cards.

**Invite friends** provides **Share link** and **Copy link**. After an eligible invited friend completes missions on three different days, both accounts receive seven days of full access, subject to the five-reward cap and eligibility checks. This is an access reward rather than cash.

Under **Share cards**, open an available card, choose **Initials** or **First name**, then **Share card** or **Save image**. Initials are the default. Cards exclude email, city and journal text. Available cards depend on earned progress; native phone sharing still needs real-device acceptance testing.

## Download data or delete an account

Open **Me → Privacy and data → Download or delete my data** (`/me/data`). **Download my data** creates a JSON export. **Delete my account** requires typing `DELETE` and permanently removes the account and linked records; download anything you need first.

In local test mode these operations affect sample records. Their live-provider behavior must be verified before launch. For sign-out, use **Sign out** at the bottom of Me. Contact and legal text remain draft launch work; the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) records those limits.
